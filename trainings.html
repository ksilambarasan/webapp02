Student Careers

Bridging the Gap Between Academics & Industry

Our student internships are intensive, practice-driven programs designed to equip aspiring embedded, IoT, and VLSI engineers with industrial skills. You will work on real hardware, analyze diagnostic protocols, and write code that interfaces directly with sensors, networks, and FPGA targets. This program is designed for ECE / EEE / E&I / CSE students who want real, hands-on embedded engineering experience — not textbook exercises.
