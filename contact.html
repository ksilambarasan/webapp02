FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding our edge platform, hardware compatibility, and training cohorts.

Does TanuvinOS support offline-only execution?

Yes. TanuvinOS is engineered to execute neural inferences entirely offline. Telemetry payloads are cached locally and synchronized with the Cloud Hub once connection is restored.

Which neural network backends are supported?

Our edge compilers fully support ONNX models, TensorFlow Lite formats, and OpenVINO optimization frameworks. Models can be quantized down to 8-bit or 4-bit integers.

Do you supply developer boards for trainings?

Yes. All students enrolled in our academy courses receive a physical developer sandbox kit shipped directly to their address, containing compute boards and core sensors.

Can we consult for custom hardware design?

Absolutely. Our electrical engineering team specializes in custom PCB layout routing, thermal modeling, and IP-rated enclosures for custom sensors.
