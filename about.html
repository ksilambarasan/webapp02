Who We Are

Who We Are

Founded in 2026, Tanuvin Technologies exists to solve the challenges that slow innovation.

We partner with product companies, startups, engineering teams, and academic institutions to transform complex ideas into reliable, market-ready solutions. From resolving critical technical bottlenecks to building complete products from the ground up, we bring deep expertise, practical execution, and a relentless focus on delivering results.

Our capabilities span Embedded Linux, RTOS, Industrial IoT, FPGA, VLSI, Product Engineering, Engineering Consulting, and Technical Training. We help organizations accelerate development, reduce risk, strengthen engineering capabilities, and create reusable technology platforms that power future innovation.

At Tanuvin Technologies, we believe that every challenge has a solution, every constraint can inspire innovation, and the right expertise can turn ambitious ideas into successful products.
