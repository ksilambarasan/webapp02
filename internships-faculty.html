FDP Overview

Modernizing Education with Industry Realities

Our Faculty Development Internship (FDP) is structured to support educators in engineering colleges and research institutions. By introducing advanced industrial design tools, firmware configurations, and kernel configurations, we help professors build modern, highly practical lab modules and curriculum syllabi that prepare students for competitive careers.

FDP Program Details

Mode: 100% Online — Live Interactive Sessions

Duration: 1 Hour per Day / 5 Days a Week

Length Options: 3 Days (Intensive) | 5 Days (Full FDP) | 10 Days (Advanced)

Target Audience: ECE / EEE / E&I / CSE Engineering Faculty

MoU support: Available for long-term college partnerships

Start Date: Batches opening soon — contact us to schedule your college's FDP

What Faculty Receive

  • Live daily online sessions (1 hr/day)
  • Study material & reference notes
  • Source code and lab exercise files
  • Access to session recordings (30 days)
  • Hands-on assignment files for classroom use
  • FDP Completion Certificate (issued by Tanuvin Technologies LLP)
  • Industry case studies for classroom use
  • Customizable curriculum on request
  • MoU support for long-term partnership
  • Post-FDP support for 2 weeks
FDP Specializations

Syllabus & Lab Integrations

Three core domains we help professors integrate into university curriculums and student labs.

VLSI Design & FPGA

Modernize digital logic simulation and physical flashing labs. Covers Verilog/VHDL RTL, testbenches, ModelSim functional simulation, Vivado, and FPGA target implementation.

ModelSim Intel Quartus Xilinx Vivado Altera Cyclone Verilog/VHDL

Embedded RTOS & IoT

Structure industrial IoT labs and real-time connectivity classes. Covers task scheduling, FreeRTOS/Zephyr bootloaders, BLE/Wi-Fi communication, and cloud integration.

STM32 ESP32 Nordic nRF FreeRTOS Zephyr RTOS

Embedded Linux & BSP

Integrate SoC board configurations into curricula. Covers Yocto Project layers, custom device drivers, root file systems, secure boot, and hardware debuggers.

BeagleBone Raspberry Pi NXP i.MX8M Yocto Project U-Boot
